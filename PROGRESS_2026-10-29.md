## 1. place_container (rammp-kitchen-capability-engines)
- **Actions** (dry run unless the node runs with `allow_execute:=true` and the goal has `"execute": true`):
  - `place` {x, y, table_z | the marked spot};
  - `mark` (fingertips → spot);
  - `retreat`;
  - `reset` {save, empty}.
- **Files:**
  - `plan.py`: planning on the shared sim, in `arm_mount`.
  - `lower.py`: the arm side.
  - `node.py`: the actions.
- **How the lowering works:**
  - settle: an `execute_joint_trajectory` impedance hold;
  - the line down: `move_joints` in impedance. Its monitor cancels on vz contact (−1 cm/s);
  - then a hold 3 mm below the contact point;
  - open, then retreat with `move_joints`.
- **Details of the descent:**
  - `move_joints` times the line minimum-jerk, so contact only counts while the commanded speed is
    ≥ 1.5 cm/s (`contact_gate`);
  - the speed scale puts ~2 cm/s at the marked table (`descent_speed_scale`);
  - one custom gain set (`impedance_kq/zeta/torque_limit` = `rammp_arm.IMPEDANCE`), not the `medium`
    profile.
- **A change to `self.arm` was tried, then reverted on request:** an `execute_joint_trajectory` monitor
  that replaces the goal instead of cancelling it. **The cancel-then-hold version worked on flatbot.**
  The recordings were not reviewed for a spring-up after the cancel.
- **Speeds are 2× RAMMP-table's,** except the lowering (`lower_speed` 2 cm/s):
  - `go_to_speed_scale` 0.4;
  - `adjust_speed_scale` 1.0;
  - `retreat_speed` 6 cm/s;
  - `reset_speed_scale` 1.0.
- **Fixes from the day's runs:**
  - **Spots too close:** the plan refuses one where the line down can't reach the table. At 0.54 m it
    could only go 4 cm down; at 0.71 m, 14 cm. Use 0.80–0.95 m.
  - **Other arm postures:** if the line can't reach, other postures are searched (`find_posture`).
    One is only used with `allow_reconfigure:=true`, via `go_to_joint_config`, which has no level
    hold. For 0.71 m no posture reached.
  - **The line down is planned again from the measured joints after the carrying moves:**
    go_to_ee_pose lets cuRobo choose the posture.
  - **The gripper open waits up to 3 s and is sent twice.**
  - **reset skips the container-tilt check** with an open gripper, or with `{"empty": true}`. It had
    refused with "the container tilts 10 deg".
  - The Dockerfile runs `rosdep update`. The example's Dockerfile lacks it: tell Swapnil.

## 2. Scene camera detection (RAMMP-table `table_detect.py`)
All of these can be overridden from the environment:
- **`TABLE_OBSTACLE_MIN_POINTS` 3 / `TABLE_OBSTACLE_MIN_HEIGHT` 0.02 m:** an object needs 3 points
  2 cm up in a 5 mm cell. Single points 1 cm up made yellow streaks over clear table.
- **`TABLE_FILL_HOLES_CM2` 25:** holes in the tabletop that touch no object count as table. A hole next
  to an object (its shadow, or a dark object) stays blocked; `test_unknown_space` still passes.
- **`TABLE_PLANE_TOL` (default 0.02 m):** the tolerance for counting a point as tabletop. 0.025 passes
  all tests; at 0.03 a crowded table's plane tilted 10.5° (a test failed). The near corner of the
  view drops out at 0.02.
- **`TABLE_EDGE_MARGIN` (default 0.08 m):** with `0.065`, `look` needs 12.0 cm of clear table around
  the spot instead of 13.5. The camera sees a narrow strip.
- **Tests:** `test_placement.py` 10/10; `test_spot_vote.py` 15/15, plus the `px` prompt test.

## 3. Choosing the spot on the camera
- `py place_bowl.py look --no-window --no-check`. Then:
  1. in Foxglove, open the depth image (`/scene_camera/depth/image_rect_raw`, rotation 0°);
  2. hover over the spot;
  3. type `px U V`, then `c`.
- It prints the `act place '{"x": .., "y": .., "table_z": ..}'` line for place_container.
- `--no-check`: no clearance, object or edge check. The person picking keeps the spot clear.
- The spot is saved in `~/.table_place/spot.json`. The same format works if copied to
  `~/.place_container/spot.json`.

- **The camera reads 3–12 cm short.** A shift alone leaves up to 5.7 cm of error. A **−12.2° turn about
  z** plus a shift of (+15.5, +15.3) cm fits all four within ~2 cm. So the scene_camera → arm_mount
  rotation in the lab's `static_tf` is ~12° off, or the camera was turned. `look` uses that TF as is:
  there is no calibration of our own.
- **The arm's fingertip heights put the table at 6.4° tilt in `arm_mount`:**
  z = 0.225 − 0.072 x − 0.086 y. That would mean either the table slopes or flatbot's `mount.yaml`
  (30° pitch) doesn't match the real mount. **Check the table with a level.**
- **The camera's table height** was 0.216–0.218 throughout; the marks gave 0.178–0.212.
- **Effect seen:** a place with `table_z` from an earlier mark (0.196) at a spot 30 cm away stopped
  3 cm "too high" (EARLY_STOP refused; the table was ~0.226 there).
