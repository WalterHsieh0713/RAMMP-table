## 4. The arm frame is tilted ~7°
Contact heights fit `z = 0.290 + 0.098 x + 0.080 y` in `arm_mount` (rms 3 mm, 10 contacts): the
table "rises" 5.6° outward and 4.5° to the left. The picker's camera view says 7.2–7.3°. The table is
level (Walter checked), so `arm_mount` is tilted: `config/robots/gen_1_5/mount.yaml` (30° pitch, no
roll) is off by roughly 4–6° each way. It likely also explains the uneven sag and the force estimate's
steps (gravity compensation uses the mount). Until it's fixed: `early_stop` 6 cm covers it; after
it's fixed, re-mark and re-save the start (every stored number is in the tilted frame).
