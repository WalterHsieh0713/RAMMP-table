## 1. Choosing the spot by clicking (`place_bowl.py look`)
Before, `look` showed the camera's one pick and you could only Confirm, Rescan or Cancel it.

Now:
- **Click the table in the picture** to put the bowl there instead. The click is turned into a
  point on the table plane (`spot_vote.click_to_table`: the pixel's ray meets the plane the
  detector found).
- **The point is checked like the camera's own pick:**
  - the bowl must fit there (lip included, clear of objects and the table edge) in at least
    `MIN_INLIER_FRAC` (60%) of the frames, not just one;
  - it must be in reach (`TABLE_REACH`).
- If it passes, the green circle moves there ("CHOSEN: …") and Confirm saves it (`source:
  "look-chosen"`). If not, a red X and the reason; click again.
- **It also works when the camera found no spot itself** (the Oct 8 case: clear table only inside
  the reach ring). `table_detect` now keeps the free-space grid (`result["grid"]`) even when no
  spot fits.
- **No display (plain ssh):** type `at X Y` at the prompt (level frame = `arm_mount`, m: x forward,
  y left of the arm base). Same check.
- After Confirm, `place --execute` goes there. A chosen spot still gets the check that nothing is
  in the hand's path on the way down; only `mark` / `--at` skip it.
- **Tests:** `test_spot_vote.py`, 15/15:
  - a click lands on the table point under it;
  - the grid is kept when nothing fits;
  - `at X Y` is understood at the prompt;
  - a refused pick is drawn as a red X.

## 3. Network notes (for whoever sets up next)
- The laptop reaches the Jetson only through the **USB Ethernet adapter** set to `192.168.1.111/24`.
  Wi-Fi (Pitt Guest) can't reach it.
- **Check the link first:** `Get-NetAdapter "Ethernet 3" | Select Status, LinkSpeed` should be **Up,
  1 Gbps**. "Disconnected, 0 bps" = no signal on the cable (cable, port, or the chair's switch
  unpowered), not an IP problem.
- **Restarting the Jetson:** ask the lab first (shared computer). Use its Reset button, the single
  circular arrow (not the two-arrow one, that's Force Recovery). It takes 1–2 min to boot. A reboot
  deletes no files.
