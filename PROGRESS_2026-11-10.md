  snorlax's was older than the one place_container uses (`JointTarget` missing).
- **The image's base was too old:** `rammp-base:nightly-20261027` has no `GoToEEPoses`. Now

  driver (`nightly-20261110`; the differences are comments).
- **Bring up the `flatbot` profile, not single nodes.** With only `arm` running (someone had started
  nodes by hand, `profile <none>`), `mark` failed with `StateTimeout`: the nightly driver publishes
  `/ee_state` only once `arm_state_publisher` is up. The planner also needs the profile's override
  (the chair, seat and person volumes).
- **Work inside tmux** (`tmux new -s place`, window 0 the node, window 1 `act`). See section 5.
- **An arm fault blocks every goal** until `/clear_faults`. The driver had restarted and come up
  with `ArmLink: faulted: the arm reports a fault` (`rt: n=0`). The node first blamed control
  (`supervisor rejected planned trajectory`), but `/control_status` said `owned: false`,
  `rejected_count: 0`. `ros2 service call /clear_faults std_srvs/srv/Trigger` → `running`.
  Read `docker logs --tail 60 sheppy-arm` first next time.
- The gripper worked all session (the Oct 29 freeze did not come back).

## 2. place_container now (defaults)
- **Carry:** adjust → lift → transit → approach, ONE `go_to_joint_configs` motion through our planned
  joints, blended (`carry_targets: joints`). The dry run checks every joint leg with cuRobo.
- **Approach (`lower_from` 0.06 m):** straight down, in position mode, a waypoint every 4 cm, to 6 cm
  above where the container's bottom meets the table.
- **Lowering:** the rest of the line in impedance, ~2 cm/s at the table, contact = vz back up to
  −1 cm/s, armed after 8 cm (`contact_ignore_m`) but at most 1 cm above where the table should be.
  No settle (`settle_s` 0). The planned line is reused when the arm ends on the planned joints.
- **Release:** hold 3 mm below the contact, open, **drop 1 cm** (`release_drop`), then back out with
  no lift.
- **Retreat:** straight back 8 cm (or ≥ 4.8 cm, past the lip). If less fits: as far back as fits,
  then a **60° turn of the hand about the wrist** (seen from above) that swings the fingertips off
  the lip. 10 cm/s at the fingertips.
- **Speeds:** `go_to_speed_scale` 0.6, `retreat_speed` 0.10.
- **reset `big_turn`** (empty hand only): allows a joint move over 120°, to recover from another
  posture.

## 3. Problems found and fixed (in order)
1. **Retreat refused at 0.81 m:** `GOAL_IN_LIMIT_MARGIN: arm_joint_4 is 0.000 rad from its limit`.
   Our IK let J4 sit ON its limit (2.570), so the straight retreat looked complete; the driver
   refuses anything within 0.1 rad. **Fix:** IK keeps 0.1 rad inside every limit.
2. **False contact 3 cm into the lowering** (empty hand, 19 cm above the table → refused, nothing let
   go). Recording `descent_20261110_181135.csv`:
   - the hand stuck for ~2 s while the slow command started, then caught up at −1.5 to −2.7 cm/s;
   - it reached the command at 3 cm, braked to −0.81 / −0.86 cm/s, just as contact armed (3 cm)
     and the speed gate opened (command 1.5 cm/s) → read as the table.
   - **Fix:** `contact_ignore_m` 0.08. Past 8 cm the hand tracked the command at −2.0 to −2.8 cm/s,
     never closer than −1.51 to the −1.0 threshold (`descent_20261110_183107.csv`).
   - `vz_stop` −0.5 was considered and rejected: on Oct 26 vz sat at ~−0.5 at a real touch.
3. **The arm flipped its posture mid-carry:** J1 +101°, J2 +70°, J3 −147°, J5 +76° from the start.
   cuRobo picks the joints for hand poses. reset then refused (`a joint moves 150 deg`); recovered
   with `big_turn`. **Fix:** the carry goes through our planned joints.
4. **Empty-gripper run never felt the table** (`went down 28 cm without stopping`). Same recording:
   - the gripper body touched 3 cm lower than the container bottom would, at 15.51 s, when the
     command had slowed to 1.49 cm/s, just under the 1.5 cm/s gate;
   - vz went −1.0 → −0.16 → 0.00: any threshold would have caught it with the gate open.
   - **Not a bug:** the lowering is tuned for the container's geometry. Test with the container.
   - The hand then pressed on (sank 8 mm, ~8 N over the offset), and the elbow ended 4 mm inside the
     lap's 2 cm cuRobo padding → reset refused (`INVALID_START_STATE_WORLD_COLLISION`); recovered
     with `reset_mode:=legacy` (straight up first, no cuRobo).
5. **The retreat swing dragged the container** (the first swing version): the fingertips, pushed
   against the container wall, slid sideways along it. **Fix:** back off as far as fits first (tips
   off the wall), drop 1 cm, no lift.
6. **0.83 m couldn't reach straight down** (22 cm, stops at the meet) in the arm's posture.
   `allow_reconfigure:=true` found another posture, but its elbow was **34 mm inside the person's lap
   volume** (cuRobo check refused). **Don't use `allow_reconfigure` on flatbot.** Use spots at
   ~0.88–0.90 m.

## 4. Measurements
- **Spring-up after the contact cancel** (open since Oct 29): +0.18 cm (vz +0.5 cm/s, then still).
  Not a problem on flatbot.
- **Press:** ~4 N per cm of command below the hand with today's gains (2 cm → ~8 N). `hold_press`
  3 mm ≈ 1 N.
- **Hand lag in the lowering:** ≤ 0.5 cm once tracking; the start of a slow command sticks for ~2 s.
- **Reach:** 0.81–0.83 m is borderline (J4 near its limit, depends on the exact start joints);
  0.88–0.90 m works. Spot used: x 0.820, y −0.148, table z 0.214 (earlier); a farther mark later.

## 5. SSH dropped three times, each right as a reset started

- **snorlax's own network stayed up:** no link or DHCP events in `journalctl` at the reset
  (only docker veth churn from someone else's containers). So it's the laptop ↔ robot Wi-Fi link.
- The node and the arm kept going (goals finish without the client). Use tmux; try Ethernet, or keep
