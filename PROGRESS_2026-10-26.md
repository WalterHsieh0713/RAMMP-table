# Progress notes: placing at a marked spot works on flatbot (snorlax)

Session: Oct 26, 2026, at HERL on **flatbot**, the robot with the arm on a 30° mount. The computer is
`snorlax` (`snorlax@snorlax.local`), Kinova Gen3 + Robotiq 2F-85. Previous notes:
`PROGRESS_2026-10-23.md`. Next: `NEXT_STEPS.md`.

## Summary
- **Placing at a chosen spot works** (empty gripper, then the bowl):
  1. `mark` with the fingertips on a sticky note;
  2. `place --execute`;
  3. the bowl lands on the sticky note.
- **The 30° mount needs nothing from us:**
  - flatbot's `mount.yaml` is `rpy_deg: [0, 30, 0]`;
  - the nightly driver gives every pose in a gravity-aligned `arm_mount`;
  - the impedance gravity model gets the tilt from the driver too.
  - There must be **no `mount.json`** in `~/.table_place/`: it would correct the tilt a second time.
- **New setup on snorlax** (section 1): `~/ros2_ws` interfaces, a venv for pybullet / open3d.
- **Contact came late:** the wrist folded about 13° after the touch. Fixed with `TABLE_VZ_STOP=-0.01`
  (section 2).
- **The retreat refused at 0.8 m.** It now takes a shorter line if that clears the lip
  (section 3).
- **The scene camera's depth is poor on the plain table** (section 4). The camera isn't integrated
  yet: `look` → `place` on flatbot is the next step.

## 2. Contact detection on the 30° mount
- **First `place --here`** (`descent_20261026_125221`), with the default `TABLE_VZ_STOP=0`:
  - the hand touched at ~7.6 s:
    - `z` flattened at ~26 cm;
    - `vz` went from −2 to ~−0.5 cm/s;
    - force z started rising.
  - `vz` never got back to 0:
    - `wy` stayed at about −3 deg/s for 4.5 s, so the wrist was folding about the fingertips;
    - over those 4.5 s the hand turned ~13°, the flange dropped another 2 cm, and the force built
      up to ~28 N;
    - contact was only detected at 12.2 s.
- **Fix:** `TABLE_VZ_STOP=-0.01` means contact = `vz` back up to −1 cm/s.
  - In free descent `vz` stays below about −1.3 cm/s; after the touch it was ~−0.5.
  - Used for the rest of the day. **Not yet the default in the code.**
- **A bump at the end** (~24–25 s): z +1 cm, `vz` −4 cm/s, ~27 N. It's where the arm leaves impedance
  mode. Not looked into yet.

## 3. Code changes
- **`place_bowl.py`, a shorter retreat if that's all that fits:**
  - From the spot at 0.80 m the 8 cm straight retreat ran out of reach 6–7 cm along, and `place` stopped with

    prints `retreat: only X cm straight back fits from here`.

  - The rest of that file still assumes the old rig (wrist-camera calibration, table z 0.135, a level
    hand), so it fails on snorlax.

    - turning the floodlight off and the dots to 1.0 made no visible difference;
